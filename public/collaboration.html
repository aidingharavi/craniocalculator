How external validation works

  1. Tell us about your cohort

    Use the form below. We'll reply to talk through fit, sample size, and timing.

  2. Complete approvals at your site

    Your IRB decides how the work is classified. Because models run locally, most projects need no transfer of patient-level data.

  3. Run the model locally

    We send the model and a data dictionary. You apply it to your cohort inside your own environment.

  4. Share aggregate metrics only

    The validation script produces discrimination, calibration, and accuracy summaries. Only those numbers leave your institution.

  5. Publish together

    Results from each site are reported in a multicenter validation paper. Authorship follows ICMJE criteria and is agreed before any data work begins.
