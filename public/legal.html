Terms and privacy

Last updated November 4, 2026.

Not medical advice

CranioCalc is for research and education. Nothing on the site is medical advice, a diagnosis, or a treatment recommendation for any individual, and it doesn't replace the judgment of a qualified clinician. The calculators and prediction models are not FDA-cleared or otherwise approved medical devices. Predictions describe groups of patients similar to those a model was developed on and may not apply to a particular patient.

Accuracy

We work to implement every formula and model correctly, but errors are possible. Check important results against the cited sources. Research digest summaries are generated automatically by a language model from PubMed abstracts and can be incomplete or wrong. Meeting dates come from third parties and can change without notice.

Data sources

Article titles and abstracts are from PubMed, courtesy of the US National Library of Medicine. Models labeled as ACS NSQIP models use data from the American College of Surgeons National Surgical Quality Improvement Program. The American College of Surgeons National Surgical Quality Improvement Program and the hospitals participating in the ACS NSQIP are the source of the data used herein; they have not verified and are not responsible for the statistical validity of the data analysis or the conclusions derived by the authors.

What we collect

  • Calculators: nothing. Calculations run in your browser and the values you enter are never sent to us.
  • Digest sign-up and collaboration messages: the email address and details you submit, used only to send the digest or reply to you. We don't sell or share them. Ask us at any time to delete them.
  • Abuse prevention: to limit spam, we keep a daily count of form submissions per visitor, keyed by a one-way hash of the IP address. The raw address isn't stored, and the count expires within about a day.
  • Hosting: the site is served by Cloudflare, which processes standard request data to deliver and protect the site. We don't use analytics or advertising cookies.
