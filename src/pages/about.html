About craniocalc

Calculators and new research for people who care for children with craniofacial differences, and for those who study it.

Who runs it

craniocalc is built and maintained by Aidin, a medical student at Mayo Clinic Alix School of Medicine whose research focuses on pediatric craniofacial and reconstructive surgery, surgical outcomes, and machine learning.

This is an independent project. Views and content are my own and are not reviewed or endorsed by Mayo Clinic.

The calculators

There are three kinds:

  • Established calculators implement published formulas and severity scales, such as the cranial vault asymmetry index. Each one cites its source and explains its method.
  • Our outcome models are prediction models developed from institutional data, such as CranioSTRIP for transfusion risk in craniosynostosis surgery.
  • ACS NSQIP models are risk models built from the American College of Surgeons National Surgical Quality Improvement Program, which records 30-day outcomes from participating hospitals across the United States.

Every prediction model is published with a model card: what it predicts, the data it was built from, how it performed in validation, and where it shouldn't be used. Models stay labeled as in development until they have been validated, and where possible the full equation is shown alongside the calculator.

New craniofacial research

Each Monday, a script pulls the previous week's new PubMed records matching craniofacial, cleft, and related terms. A language model (Anthropic's Claude) selects the papers most relevant to clinicians and researchers, favoring trials, large cohorts, and systematic reviews, and writes a one- or two-sentence summary of each from its abstract. Summaries are published without manual review, so always read the paper.

Principles

  • Show the method and source for every number.
  • State limitations next to results, not in fine print.
  • Collect no patient information. Calculators run entirely in your browser.
  • No advertising or sponsored content.
