About CranioCalc

Calculators and new research for people who care for children with craniofacial differences, and for those who study it.

Who Runs It

CranioCalc is maintained by Aidin Gharavi, a medical student whose research focuses on pediatric craniofacial and reconstructive surgery.

This is an independent project, and no content published on this website is reviewed or endorsed by any institution.

Calculator Information

CranioCalc has multiple calculators, including established formulas and scoring systems, single-center prediction models, and prediction models built from multicenter data such as the ACS National Surgical Quality Improvement Program (NSQIP). Each calculator cites the study it comes from.
