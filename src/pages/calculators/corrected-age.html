Corrected Age

Chronological, corrected, and postmenstrual age for an infant born preterm.

weeks
days

Defaults to today.

Method

Corrected age = chronological age − (40 weeks − gestational age at birth)

Postmenstrual age = gestational age at birth + chronological age

Chronological age is the time elapsed since birth. Corrected age is the age the child would be if born at 40 weeks, and is the preferred term for children up to 3 years of age who were born preterm. Postmenstrual age is used during the perinatal period, typically while the infant is in hospital.

In craniofacial practice, corrected age is used to interpret head growth and head shape measurements against age norms and to time interventions such as cranial orthosis treatment.

Source: Engle WA; American Academy of Pediatrics Committee on Fetus and Newborn. Age terminology during the perinatal period. Pediatrics. 2004;114(5):1362–1364. Find on PubMed.
